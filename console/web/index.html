How this vault works, in detail

Start here

Castlekeep lets you trade on an exchange while your coins stay in a vault that needs your signature to move. If the exchange disappears or cheats, you can still take your coins back on your own after a waiting period. Everything here uses test coins with no value.

    Your signet address. Get free signet coins from a public signet faucet (search "signet faucet") and send them here. They have no value.

    Your protection

    It watches exits for you and submits your newest signed state if an older one shows up.

    Exchange

    The order book and the other key holder.

    –

    One order: sell from this vault and buy another coin with the money, at the prices shown. No cash is used.

    The exchange signs the new state for the coin you buy first. Only then do you sign the sale, so you never give up one coin without already holding the other.

    It matches the best opposite order first, at that order's price. Until it fills, the coins (or cash) it needs are set aside. Your wallet signs each fill only after checking it against this order: same coin and side, not more than you asked for, at your price or better.
